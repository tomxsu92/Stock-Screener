How the score and the plan work

Potential score (0–100) = 25% Trend + 25% Momentum + 20% Growth + 15% Value + 15% Analyst upside. Trend checks whether price is above the 50- and 200-day averages and whether the 50-day is rising. Momentum, growth (revenue & earnings growth), value (PEG, or forward P/E) and upside (analyst average target vs. price) are ranked against every other stock in the list, so a 90 means "better than 90% of this list".

Signals: Buy zone uptrend and price has pulled back near its 20/50-day averages · Uptrend healthy, but a dip gives a better entry · Wait overbought/extended · Watch mixed trend · Avoid downtrend.

Plan: entry zone = 20–50 day averages in an uptrend (or reclaiming the 200-day otherwise); stop = 2× ATR (average daily range) below the zone; target 1 = analyst average target (or 2× your risk); target 2 = 3× risk. Hold time depends on whether the idea is trend/growth, momentum or value driven.
